After hours: hardware and IoT

I like systems I can hold. My side projects sit where backend engineering meets electronics: firmware, file formats, sync protocols and PCBs.

E-ink Quran and library reader

[IN PROGRESS]

An offline-first e-ink reader built on an ESP32-S3, with a desktop app that converts books and sends them to the device. It works with no internet at all; Wi-Fi and Bluetooth are only for setup and sync.

  • Firmware for the e-paper display, SD card, buttons and rotary wheel, and deep sleep and wake, brought up and verified on real hardware.
  • A custom binary content format with a parser on the device and a TypeScript reader/writer on desktop, kept byte-identical by a shared test fixture.
  • A converter that turns PDF, EPUB and text into validated packages, with a preview before install.
  • Resumable uploads with atomic install, tested against power loss at every write, plus Bluetooth first-boot setup.
  • A Tauri desktop app with library, device, converter and photo tabs.
  • Doubles as a table clock: Wi-Fi time sync and a rotating greyscale photo on the home screen.
